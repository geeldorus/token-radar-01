    The model, in full (radar-3)

    implied = size base × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth × young discount

    • Size base: TVL⅓ × $1B⅔ — TVL through its cube root, anchored at $1B, so the base equals TVL at $1B. A giant above $1B is compressed, a small protocol below $1B is lifted: size still matters, but it no longer decides the board on its own. Radar-2 multiplied TVL straight through, and its board was nearly a TVL ranking (Spearman 0.96 against TVL rank, 18 of the top 20 the 20 largest protocols). Radar-3's board is not (Spearman 0.71; 115 of 198 rows move ten or more places against radar-2, while the top stays the large, long-running protocols).
    • Category multiple: exchanges and derivatives 0.38, lending and CDP 0.36, yield and farming 0.32, RWA and basis trading 0.30, curators and allocators 0.28, staking 0.24, anything else 0.18. A dollar of TVL is worth more where the protocol takes a fee.
    • Growth: 1 + 0.8 × seven-day change, clamped to −50%…+200% (0.60–2.60).
    • Maturity: 0.65 + age ÷ 1460 days × 0.60 (0.65–1.25); unknown age scores 0.90. A protocol that has run for years with no token is overdue one. Under 90 days old, × 0.80.
    • Breadth: 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, up to 8 (1.00–1.16).
    • Scope: the board ranks every pre-token protocol the watchlist rule keeps — the 40 watchlist slugs and every other one that is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. Wrapped and staked products (WBTC, staked ETH, bridged USDT) are excluded, because their token already exists.
